# Website Vision

This website is both a personal brand and a portfolio.

Its purpose is to tell the story of Nattapong through projects, achievements, and personal growth, rather than simply displaying work.

Visitors should immediately understand that this is the personal website of Nattapong (`nattapong.dev`).

The website should feel like exploring my journey as an engineer and as a person.

Story cards are the core of the experience. Each card represents a chapter of my journey, such as:

- University basketball player
- Engineering projects
- Personal milestones
- Technical achievements
- Learning journey

At this stage, the Home page is the primary focus.

The small rectangular cards act as story and project previews that guide visitors to explore more.

Since personal photos are not available yet, use high-quality, royalty-free developer-related imagery as temporary placeholders.

Do not design other pages yet.
Do not define animation or motion behavior yet.
The website should introduce me before it introduces my work.
Every design decision should reinforce the feeling that visitors are learning who I am.
